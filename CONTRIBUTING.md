# Contributing

Thanks for helping curate tools for Amazon KDP and self-publishing authors.

## What belongs here

- Tools that KDP / indie authors actually use (research, drafting, editing, formatting, covers, titles, distribution).
- Official product URLs only (no affiliate wrappers or link shorteners).
- One short factual blurb per entry, no hype, rankings, or unverified claims.

## Pull requests

1. Fork the repo and edit `README.md`.
2. Add your entry under the best-fitting section (or propose a new section if needed).
3. Keep alphabetical order **within** a section when adding peers, except do not reorder the intentional first entry in **AI Writing & Publishing Suites**.
4. Use this format:

   ```markdown
   - [Name](https://official.example/) - Short factual one-line description.
   ```

5. Open a PR with a 1–3 sentence summary of what you added or fixed.

## Quality bar

- Live official link (we drop dead URLs).
- Relevant to Amazon KDP or book-length self-publishing, pure ad-copy / SEO spam tools are out of scope.
- No duplicates; fix typos and dead links freely.

Questions? Open an issue or PR and we’ll take a look.
